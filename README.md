# Enhancing-Post-Stroke-Function
Enhancing Function for Veterans Post-Stroke at the System and Individual Level 

With Dr. Kim Waddell 
Identified Veterans hospitalized with a primary diagnosis of stroke using ICD-10 codes in VA medical records or CMS billing records. Matched hospitalizations to post-acute care episodes and standardized Medicare assessments to assess functional status at post-acute care discharge. Analyses are in progress but they will describe the variation in functional status recovery and post-acute care utilizaiton patterns among Medicare-aged Veterans. The ultimate goal is to quantify the degree of functional impairment at discharge from post-acute stroke rehabilitation and identify sub-populations who may experience greater impairment.
